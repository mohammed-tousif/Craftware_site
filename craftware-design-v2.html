Quba International

A full booking website for a Hajj, Umrah and Ziyarat travel agency in Hubli — package browsing, a community and blog section, an FAQ, and a callback-request form connected straight to WhatsApp. We also run their ongoing marketing: Meta and Facebook ads, SEO, and WhatsApp automation for booking enquiries.

Kaksha

A multi-tenant attendance and leave management platform for schools and colleges — five levels of role-based access from Super Admin down to Student, per-subject attendance tracking, CSV bulk roster upload, and a self-service leave workflow. Built to replace paper registers and disconnected spreadsheets with one auditable system.

Web developmentLive projectFull-stack build

Her Shield

A women's safety platform built around real-time protection — live location tracking with trusted contacts, one-tap SOS alerts over SMS, WhatsApp and email, a safety map, and a community space. Firebase Auth on the front end, an Express and Firestore backend, Socket.IO powering the live tracking rooms.

Web developmentLive projectFull-stack build
